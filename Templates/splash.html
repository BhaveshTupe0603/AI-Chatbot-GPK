Help & Support
❓ How do I book a ride?
Simply type "Book a ride" in the chat, provide your pickup and drop locations, choose a ride type, and confirm.
❓ How is the fare calculated?
Fare is based on distance, ride type, and base rates. An estimated fare is shown before booking.
❓ What if I face an issue during or after the ride?
You can open the complaint form in the chatbot by saying "I have a complaint". Our support team will review it.
❓ How can I cancel a ride? Will I be charged?
Type "Cancel ride [Ride ID]" to cancel. No charges apply for cancellations before the ride starts.
❓ Is my location shared during a trip?
Yes, but only with your assigned driver for navigation purposes. It is never shared with anyone else.
❓ How does the 'Refer and Earn' work?
Share your referral code with friends. Once they complete their first ride, you earn credits for discounts.
